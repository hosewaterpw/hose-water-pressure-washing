# Updating your website

This guide is for Jon. It covers everything you can change on the site yourself,
without touching any code.

You can update:

- **Gallery photos** — add new before/after shots, remove old ones
- **Services** — names, descriptions, photos
- **FAQ** — questions and answers
- **Contact details** — phone, email, opening hours
- **Social links** — Google, Facebook, Instagram

---

## Logging in

1. Go to **hosewaterpw.com/admin**
2. Click the login button and sign in.

Bookmark that page. It is the only address you need.

The first time you log in you may be asked to set a password from an email
invitation. After that it remembers you on that device.

> This page is hidden from Google. It will never show up in search results, and
> nobody can get in without a login.

---

## Important: changes take a minute or two to appear

When you hit **Publish**, the website rebuilds itself in the background. Your
change goes live **about 1–2 minutes later**.

So if you publish a photo and immediately refresh the site and don't see it —
that's normal. Wait two minutes and refresh again.

This delay is what keeps the site fast for your customers, so it's worth it.

## Adding a gallery photo

1. Click **Gallery photos** in the left-hand menu.
2. Click **New Photo** (top right).
3. Fill in the boxes:

| Box | What to put |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Where the job was, e.g. `House Wash in Wells, ME` |
| **Photo** | Click the box, then **Upload** and pick the file |
| **Photo description for screen readers** | Describe what's in the picture, e.g. `Vinyl siding before and after pressure washing in Wells, Maine` |
| **Category** | Pick from the dropdown — this decides which filter it shows under |
| **Show on homepage** | Leave **off** unless you want it in the small strip on the front page |

4. Click **Publish**. If a small menu appears, choose **Publish now**.

### About that "screen readers" box

It sounds technical, but it matters for two reasons: blind visitors' software
reads it out loud, and **Google reads it too**. A photo described as
"Vinyl siding before and after pressure washing in Wells, Maine" helps you show
up when someone in Wells searches for house washing. A photo described as
"IMG_4471" does nothing for you.

Write it like you're describing the photo to someone on the phone.

### About "Show on homepage"

The homepage strip only has room for a handful of photos. Pick your **best 4**
and turn this on for those. Everything else still appears on the Gallery page —
turning it off doesn't hide a photo, it just keeps it off the front page.

## Photo tips

**Rename the file before you upload it.** `house-wash-wells-me.jpg` is better
than `IMG_4471.jpg`. Google reads file names. Use dashes, not spaces.

**Size.** Phone photos are often 8–15 MB. The site shrinks them automatically,
but smaller is still better. **Under 1600 pixels wide and under 500 KB is
ideal.** If you're emailing photos to yourself off your phone, choose "Medium"
or "Large" rather than "Actual Size" — that alone usually does the job.

**Before/after shots** work best side by side in a single image, which is how
the current ones are set up.

## Removing a gallery photo

1. **Gallery photos** → click the one you want gone.
2. Scroll to the bottom and click **Delete entry**.
3. Confirm.

It disappears from the site on the next rebuild (1–2 minutes).

## Editing a service

1. Click **Services** in the left menu.
2. Click the service you want to change.
3. Edit whatever you like, then **Publish**.

The boxes:

| Box | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Service name** | The heading, e.g. `Roof Cleaning` |
| **Photo** | The picture on the Services page |
| **Full description** | The paragraph on the Services page |
| **Short name (homepage card)** | Optional. Leave blank and it uses the service name |
| **Short description (homepage card)** | Optional. Keep it under 140 characters so the homepage cards stay the same height |
| **Key points** | The bullet points. Click **Add Point** for another one |
| **Gallery category** | Which gallery filter the "View Gallery" button jumps to |

To add a whole new service, click **New Service** instead. To remove one, open
it and click **Delete entry** at the bottom.

## Editing the FAQ

1. Click **FAQ** in the left menu.
2. Click a question to edit it, or **New Question** to add one.
3. **Section** is a dropdown — it decides which heading the question is grouped
   under on the FAQ page. Pick the closest fit.
4. **Publish**.

FAQs are worth keeping up to date. When customers ask you the same thing twice
on the phone, that's a question worth adding here — people search for those
exact phrases, and answering them on the page brings those searches to you.

## Changing your phone number, email or hours

1. Click **Site settings** in the left menu.
2. Click **Contact information**.
3. Change what you need, then **Publish**.

Two things to be careful with:

**There are two phone number boxes.** Change *both*.
- *Phone number (as shown)* is what visitors read: `(207) 370-8667`
- *Phone number (for tap-to-call)* is what happens when someone taps it on a
  phone. It must be written `+12073708667` — plus sign, country code, digits
  only, no spaces or brackets.

**There are two sets of hours.** Change *both*.
- *Opening hours (as shown)* is the text on your Contact page. Write it however
  reads best, one line per row.
- *Opening time*, *Closing time* and *Days open* are what **Google** reads to
  show your hours in search results. Use 24-hour times: `08:00` and `17:00`.

## Social links

**Site settings** → **Social links**.

Paste the full web address, starting with `https://`. These show in your footer
and they also tell Google which Facebook and Google Business pages belong to
you, which helps your listing.

A wrong link here is worse than no link, so leave a box empty if you're not sure.

## If something looks wrong

**I published but the site hasn't changed.**
Wait two minutes and refresh. If it's still not there after five minutes, force
a fresh load: **Ctrl+Shift+R** on Windows, **Cmd+Shift+R** on Mac.

**My photo is sideways.**
Rotate it on your phone or computer first, then re-upload. Save the rotated copy
as a new file — some phones only *tag* a photo as rotated rather than actually
rotating it, and websites ignore that tag.

**Publish button won't work.**
Something required is empty. Look for a red outline around a box near the top of
the form.

**I deleted something by mistake.**
Nothing is ever really gone — every change is recorded. Get in touch and it can
be put back.

---

## What not to change

Everything in the admin area is safe to edit — you can't break the site from
there. The two things to leave alone:

- **Country** under Contact information (`US`)
- The **admin address itself** — don't share `/admin` publicly

If you want a change that isn't in this list — new page, different colours,
new section — that needs a developer.
